# Equity derivatives flow: author question outline

- Who buys autocallables, and what objectives or constraints motivate them?
- Which risks does the investor retain, including issuer credit and capital loss?
- How does a dealer hedge delta, vega and gamma through the note's life?
- What happens to delta and gamma near autocall and knock-in barriers?
- How do discrete monitoring and observation dates affect the hedge?
- What happens when the underlying gaps across a barrier?
- What does skew do to put-spread and digital prices, and why?
- How do funding, dividends and correlation affect a quoted structured product?
- What is dispersion trading, and what positions express implied correlation?
- Which assumptions in this project's models differ from dealer practice?
- Which sources have you read, and which claims can you defend in your own words?
